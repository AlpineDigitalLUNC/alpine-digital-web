The rise, fall & recovery of Terra.

From a $40 billion algorithmic stablecoin empire to the largest collapse in crypto history — and the community that refused to let the chain die.

Five phases, forty milestones, every key source documented. This is the factual backbone of the Alpine Digital thesis — read it before the models, and judge the evidence yourself.

Phase I — 2018 to April 2022

The Rise

Four years from a Seoul white paper to the fourth-largest stablecoin in the world — built on a mechanism that had never been stress-tested at scale.

Jan 2018

Terraform Labs founded

Do Kwon (Stanford CS, ex-Apple/Microsoft) and Daniel Shin (TMON co-founder) establish TFL in Seoul. The white paper proposes a decentralised stablecoin on the Cosmos SDK.

Aug 2018

Binance backs the Terra Alliance

TFL secures early funding. The Terra Alliance — 15 e-commerce companies across Asia representing 45 million users — is established.

Jan 2019

$62M ICO & LUNA launch

$62M raised, LUNA sold at $0.80. Initial supply: 1 billion. First recorded price: $3.27 on May 7, 2019.

Jul 2019

Chai payment partnership

Terra partners with Chai, a Korean mobile-payments app; e-commerce purchases are processed via the Terra blockchain. Later central to the fraud charges.

Jan 2020

Daniel Shin departs

Shin leaves TFL after two years. Kwon takes 91.7% ownership. LUNA trades at $0.20–$0.50.

Jun 2020

Anchor Protocol white paper

"The gold standard for passive income on the blockchain." Promises ~20% APY on UST deposits — and eventually absorbs 75% of all circulating UST.

Sep 2020

UST stablecoin launches

TerraUSD (UST) — an algorithmic stablecoin pegged to the dollar via mint-and-burn with LUNA. No fiat reserves.

Jan–Jul 2021

VC flood: $200M+ raised

$25M (Jan) and $150M (Jul) from Arrington Capital, Coinbase Ventures, Galaxy Digital, Lightspeed and Pantera. LUNA climbs from $0.50 into double digits.

May 2021

First UST depeg — the hidden bailout

UST drops below $0.92. Kwon claims the algorithm "self-healed." Prosecutors later reveal Jump Trading secretly restored the peg — central to the fraud charges.

Sep 2021

Columbus-5 upgrade

Post-upgrade, all swapped LUNA is burned (versus 5% previously). More deflationary — but it removes the community-funding safety valve.

Nov 2021

Community pool burned for UST

88.675M LUNA swapped for UST. Supply balloons from 2.5B to 6.5B. Swaps incur 30% fees, boosting staking yield to 10%.

Jan 2022

Luna Foundation Guard established

A Singapore non-profit with Kwon as director. It accumulates 80,394 BTC (~$2.4B) to "defend the UST peg."

Apr 1, 2022

4pool announcement

A new liquidity pool with Frax Finance and Redacted Cartel. The transition leaves Curve's 3pool illiquid — and vulnerable.

Apr 5, 2022

LUNA hits $119.18 — the all-time high

The ecosystem exceeds $50B in market cap. UST is the 4th-largest stablecoin at $18B. Anchor holds $17B+ in TVL.

Phase II — May 7–13, 2022

The Fall

Six days. A $45 billion ecosystem unwinds through the exact mechanism that built it.

May 7
evening

The trigger: 3pool liquidity drain

TFL withdraws 150M UST from Curve's 3pool. Within 13 minutes a trader swaps 85M UST for USDC; a second converts 100M in tranches. TFL withdraws another 100M. UST slips below $0.99.

May 8

LFG deploys the Bitcoin reserves

LFG sells ~$2.4B of Bitcoin to buy UST. Three supporters swap $480M USDT for UST. The peg is briefly restored to ~$0.995.

May 9

The second — and final — depeg

UST loses its peg permanently. Mass Anchor withdrawals; panic cascades across Curve, the CEXs and on-chain. UST crashes 40% in hours.

May 10

LFG exhausts its reserves

Reserves depleted. The arbitrage mechanism triggers LUNA hyperinflation. Price: ~$30 to single digits.

May 11

Emergency Proposal 1164 — the death spiral

Minting capacity is raised from $293M to $1.2B daily, accelerating hyperinflation. LUNA supply: 1B to 6.5T in three days. Price: $0.31.

May 12–13

Chain halted; LUNA at zero

The chain halts twice. LUNA falls below $0.0001; UST sits at $0.08. ~$45B is wiped out — triggering Three Arrows Capital, Celsius and Voyager, and contributing to FTX's collapse.

May 16

Do Kwon proposes the fork

"Revival Plan 2": fork to Terra 2.0 (LUNA); rename the original chain Terra Classic (LUNC). The proposal passes May 25; the new chain launches May 28.

Phase III — Theories & blame

What actually happened?

Five competing explanations — weighed against the on-chain record, the academic work, and what the courts eventually proved.

Proven — court verdict

Do Kwon's fraud

The DOJ proved Kwon misled investors about the "self-healing" peg (Jump Trading secretly restored it in May 2021) and misrepresented the Chai integration. Guilty plea, August 2025. The judge: "fraud on an epic, generational scale."

Well supported

Structural inevitability

Anchor's 20% APY required $6M/day in subsidies; 75% of UST sat in one protocol; a declining LUNA cap against a growing UST supply was a reflexivity trap. An NBER paper documents the inherent instability.

Partially supported

Coordinated Curve-pool attack

Traders exploited 3pool illiquidity during the 4pool migration. Chainalysis confirmed two wallets swapped ~$185M UST for USDC — but concluded the collapse "stemmed from sustainability concerns, not targeted manipulation."

Unproven

BlackRock / Citadel conspiracy

Originated on 4chan, amplified by Charles Hoskinson: 100K BTC allegedly borrowed from Gemini, 25K swapped for UST, both dumped. All three firms categorically denied involvement.

Unproven

The regulatory angle

Theorised that Treasury Secretary Yellen had an incentive to see algorithmic stablecoins fail; she used the collapse to push regulation. No evidence of orchestration.

Phase IV — 2022 to 2025

The Legal Reckoning

From fugitive to a 15-year federal sentence — and a $4.47 billion settlement.

Feb 16, 2023

SEC charges Terraform & Kwon

Securities fraud and unregistered offerings. LUNA and MIR are ruled securities.

Mar 23, 2023

Kwon arrested in Montenegro

Intercepted at Podgorica Airport with forged Costa Rican and Belgian documents — a fugitive since September 2022.

Jan 21, 2024

Chapter 11 bankruptcy filed

Filed in Delaware. Assets and liabilities: $100M–$500M. Ownership: Kwon 92%, Shin 8%.

Apr 5, 2024

SEC civil verdict: liable for fraud

A nine-day trial and a two-hour deliberation. The jury finds both liable; Kwon is absent, in Montenegrin custody.

Jun 12, 2024

$4.47B SEC settlement

$3.587B disgorgement, $467M interest, $420M penalty. Kwon personally: $204.3M.

Sep 20, 2024

Liquidation plan approved

A Wind Down Trust is established. Estimated repayments: $185M–$442M.

Dec 31, 2024

Extradited to the United States

After a protracted US–South Korea battle. Held without bail.

Aug 2025

Kwon pleads guilty

Wire fraud and conspiracy; he admits concealing Jump Trading's role. The 9-count indictment resolves to 2 counts.

Dec 11, 2025

A 15-year prison sentence

"Fraud on an epic, generational scale." He must serve half before a South Korea transfer application, and forfeits $19M.

Phase V — June 2022 to present

Recovery & Rebuild

The part the headlines missed: a fully community-run chain, still shipping — and the reason this archive exists.

May 28, 2022

The fork — Terra Classic is born

Terra 2.0 launches. The original chain becomes Terra Classic (LUNC); UST becomes USTC. The community inherits 6.5T tokens and a broken stablecoin.

Sep 2022

Terra Rebels & the burn tax

A community group steps up: a 1.2% on-chain burn tax. Binance contributes all LUNC trading fees to burns.

Feb 2023

Blockchain upgrade v1.0.5

At block height 11,543,150. 226M LUNC burned in January alone — still short of meaningful supply-reduction targets.

Oct 2023

L1TF revival roadmap

Vinh Nguyen and the L1 Task Force set the agenda: stability, new use cases, governance, L2 onboarding. 344M LUNC returned to the community pool.

2024

A new DeFi ecosystem

Terraport, LBUN, Selenium, Garuda DEX, TerraCasino. Upgrade v3.3.0 ships. LUNC ranges $0.0001–$0.00015.

2025

Market Module reactivation & USTC staking

The "Ziggy" ERM passes with 76.4%. Market Module v3.5.0 ships in August; MM2.0 by Vegas/StrathCole follows. USTC staking arrives (Mar 2026), v3.6.1 in December; SDK v0.53 is planned for 2026.

Jan 1, 2026

Binance's 19th burn: 5.33B LUNC

A 20% price rally. Total burns pass 436B (~6.7% of supply). Circulating: ~5.4T. Price: ~$0.00006.

Where the chain stands today — 130 validators; ~15% of supply staked (~973B); USTC at $0.005 (99.5% below peg). Active teams: Vegas Node, StrathCole, BLV Labs, OrbitLabs, Duncan. Fee split: 20% burn / 20% Oracle / 60% stabilisation. What that means for the thesis is the subject of the next chapter.
