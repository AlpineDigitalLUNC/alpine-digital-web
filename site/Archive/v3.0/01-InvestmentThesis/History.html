Our Mission

Alpine Digital is a cryptocurrency investment group focused on decentralised finance. We go deep — identifying specific DeFi ecosystems where we see exceptional long-term value from both an investment returns and ecosystem growth perspective, and committing fully to building within them.

Today, our primary focus is the Luna Classic ecosystem, which we believe represents the most positively asymmetric opportunity in the digital asset space. We launched an independent validator on the network, participate actively in on-chain governance, and contribute directly to the development and strategic direction of the ecosystem.

Our audience is institutional investors, family offices, ultra-high-net-worth individuals, and venture capital firms — participants who want to evaluate this opportunity with the same rigour they apply to any other asset class. This site provides the analytical tools, market intelligence, and ecosystem context that a sophisticated investor needs to form a thesis, size a position, and engage with confidence.

Nothing on this site constitutes investment advice. You should always consult with a qualified financial advisor before making any investment decisions. This is a resource for research, for sharpening your analytical edge, and for engaging with the Luna Classic ecosystem on a deeper level.

Phase I — The Rise (2018–April 2022)

Jan 2018
Terraform Labs Founded
Do Kwon (Stanford CS, ex-Apple/Microsoft) and Daniel Shin (TMON co-founder) establish TFL in Seoul. White paper proposes decentralized stablecoin on Cosmos SDK.
Aug 2018
Binance Backs Terra Alliance
TFL secures early funding. Terra Alliance — 15 e-commerce companies across Asia representing 45 million users — established.
Jan 2019
$62M ICO & LUNA Launch
$62M raised, LUNA sold at $0.80. Initial supply: 1 billion. First recorded price: $3.27 on May 7, 2019.
Jul 2019
Chai Payment Partnership
Terra partners with Chai, a Korean mobile payments app. E-commerce purchases processed via Terra blockchain. Later central to fraud charges.
Jan 2020
Daniel Shin Departs
Shin leaves TFL after two years. Kwon takes 91.7% ownership. LUNA trades $0.20–$0.50.
Jun 2020
Anchor Protocol White Paper
"Gold standard for passive income on the blockchain." Promises ~20% APY on UST deposits. Eventually absorbs 75% of all circulating UST.
Sep 2020
UST Stablecoin Launches
TerraUSD (UST) — algorithmic stablecoin pegged to USD via mint-and-burn with LUNA. No fiat reserves.
Jan–Jul 2021
VC Flood: $200M+ Raised
$25M (Jan) and $150M (Jul) from Arrington Capital, Coinbase Ventures, Galaxy Digital, Lightspeed, Pantera. LUNA climbs $0.50 → double digits.
May 2021
First UST Depeg (Hidden Bailout)
UST drops below $0.92. Kwon claims algorithm "self-healed." Prosecutors later reveal Jump Trading secretly restored the peg — central to fraud charges.
Sep 2021
Columbus-5 Upgrade
Post-upgrade, all swapped LUNA burned (vs. 5% previously). More deflationary but removes community funding safety valve.
Nov 2021
Community Pool Burned for UST
88.675M LUNA swapped for UST. Supply balloons 2.5B → 6.5B. Swaps incur 30% fees, boosting staking yield to 10%.
Jan 2022
Luna Foundation Guard Established
Non-profit in Singapore, Kwon as director. Accumulates 80,394 BTC (~$2.4B) to "defend the UST peg."
Apr 1, 2022
4pool Announcement
New liquidity pool with Frax Finance and Redacted Cartel. Transition leaves Curve's 3pool illiquid and vulnerable.
Apr 5, 2022
LUNA Hits $119.18 All-Time High
Ecosystem exceeds $50B market cap. UST is 4th-largest stablecoin ($18B). Anchor: $17B+ TVL.

Phase II — The Fall (May 7–13, 2022)

May 7 (evening)
The Trigger: 3pool Liquidity Drain
TFL withdraws 150M UST from Curve 3pool. Within 13 min, trader swaps 85M UST→USDC. Second trader converts 100M in tranches. TFL withdraws another 100M. UST slips below $0.99.
May 8
LFG Deploys Bitcoin Reserves
LFG sells ~$2.4B Bitcoin reserves to buy UST. Three supporters swap $480M USDT for UST. Peg briefly restored to ~$0.995.
May 9
The Second (Final) Depeg
UST loses peg permanently. Mass Anchor withdrawals. Panic cascades across Curve, CEXs, on-chain. UST crashes 40% in hours.
May 10
LFG Exhausts Reserves
Reserves depleted. Arbitrage mechanism triggers LUNA hyperinflation. Price: ~$30 → single digits.
May 11
Emergency Proposal 1164 & Death Spiral
Minting capacity raised $293M → $1.2B daily. Accelerated hyperinflation. LUNA supply: 1B → 6.5T in three days. Price: $0.31.
May 12–13
Chain Halted, LUNA at Zero
Chain halted twice. LUNA below $0.0001. UST at $0.08. ~$45B wiped. Triggers Three Arrows Capital, Celsius, Voyager collapses, contributes to FTX collapse.
May 16
Do Kwon Proposes Fork
"Revival Plan 2": fork to Terra 2.0 (LUNA), rename original to Terra Classic (LUNC). Passes May 25. New chain launches May 28.

Phase III — Theories & Blame

Theory 1: BlackRock / Citadel Conspiracy
Originated on 4chan, amplified by Charles Hoskinson. Alleged: 100K BTC borrowed from Gemini, 25K swapped for UST, both dumped. All three firms categorically denied involvement.
Unproven
Theory 2: Coordinated Curve Pool Attack
Traders exploited 3pool illiquidity during 4pool migration. Chainalysis confirmed two wallets swapped ~$185M UST→USDC, but collapse "stemmed from sustainability concerns, not targeted manipulation."
Partially Supported
Theory 3: Structural Inevitability
Anchor's 20% APY required $6M/day subsidies. 75% of UST in one protocol. LUNA cap declining vs. UST supply = reflexivity trap. NBER paper documents inherent instability.
Well Supported
Theory 4: Do Kwon's Fraud (Court Verdict)
DOJ proved Kwon misled investors about self-healing (Jump Trading secretly restored May 2021 peg) and misrepresented Chai integration. Guilty plea August 2025. Judge: "fraud on an epic, generational scale."
Proven
Theory 5: Yellen / Regulatory Angle
Theorized Yellen had incentive to see algo stablecoins fail. She used collapse to push regulation. No evidence of orchestration.
Unproven

Phase IV — The Legal Reckoning (2022–2025)

Feb 16, 2023
SEC Charges Terraform & Kwon
Securities fraud and unregistered offerings. LUNA and MIR ruled securities.
Mar 23, 2023
Kwon Arrested in Montenegro
Intercepted at Podgorica Airport with fake Costa Rican and Belgian documents. Fugitive since Sep 2022.
Jan 21, 2024
Chapter 11 Bankruptcy Filed
Delaware. Assets/liabilities: $100M–$500M. Kwon 92%, Shin 8% ownership.
Apr 5, 2024
SEC Civil Verdict: Liable for Fraud
Nine-day trial, two-hour deliberation. Jury finds both liable. Kwon absent — Montenegro custody.
Jun 12, 2024
$4.47B SEC Settlement
$3.587B disgorgement + $467M interest + $420M penalty. Kwon personally: $204.3M.
Sep 20, 2024
Liquidation Plan Approved
Wind Down Trust established. Estimated repayments: $185M–$442M.
Dec 31, 2024
Extradited to United States
After protracted US/South Korea battle. Held without bail.
Aug 2025
Kwon Pleads Guilty
Wire fraud & conspiracy. Admits concealing Jump Trading's role. 9-count indictment → 2 counts.
Dec 11, 2025
15-Year Prison Sentence
"Fraud on an epic, generational scale." Must serve half before South Korea transfer application. Forfeits $19M.

Phase V — Recovery & Rebuild (June 2022–Present)

May 28, 2022
Chain Fork — Terra Classic Is Born
Terra 2.0 launches. Original chain → Terra Classic (LUNC). UST → USTC. Community inherits 6.5T tokens and broken stablecoin.
Sep 2022
Terra Rebels & Burn Tax
Community group steps up. 1.2% on-chain burn tax. Binance contributes all LUNC trading fees to burns.
Feb 2023
Blockchain Upgrade v1.0.5
Block height 11,543,150. 226M LUNC burned in January alone — short of meaningful supply reduction targets.
Oct 2023
L1TF Revival Roadmap
Vinh Nguyen and L1 Task Force: stability, new use cases, governance, L2 onboarding. Returns 344M LUNC to community pool.
2024
New DeFi Ecosystem
Terraport, LBUN, Selenium, Garuda DEX, TerraCasino. Upgrade v3.3.0. LUNC range: $0.0001–$0.00015.
2025
Market Module Reactivation & USTC Staking
"Ziggy" ERM passes 76.4%. Market Module v3.5.0 (Aug). MM2.0 by Vegas/StrathCole. USTC Staking (Mar 2026). v3.6.1 (Dec). SDK v0.53 planned 2026.
Jan 1, 2026
Binance 19th Burn: 5.33B LUNC
20% price rally. Total burns: 436B+ (~6.7% of supply). Circulating: ~5.4T. Price: ~$0.00006.
Mar 2026
Current State
130 validators. 15% staked (~973B). USTC: $0.005 (99.5% below peg). Teams: Vegas Node, StrathCole, BLV Labs, OrbitLabs, Duncan. Fee split: 20% burn / 20% Oracle / 60% stabilization.
